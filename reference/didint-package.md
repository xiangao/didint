# didint: Difference-in-Differences with Spatial Interference

Doubly robust estimators for difference-in-differences when potential
outcomes depend on neighbours' treatment status (spatial interference).
Implements the 2x2 case of Xu (2023) and the dynamic / staggered
extensions of Xu (2026), with spatial-HAC standard errors based on unit
locations.
